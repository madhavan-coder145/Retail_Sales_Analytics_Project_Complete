Retail Sales & Customer Performance Analytics

Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data to identify business trends, customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, and profitability.

The project uses SQL, Python, Excel, and Power BI to transform raw retail data into meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations.

Project Objectives

- Analyze overall retail sales and profitability.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour.
- Analyze city-wise sales performance.
- Evaluate payment method preferences.
- Identify monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Support business decisions through data-driven insights.

Dataset Information

- Dataset: Retail Sales Dataset
- Total Records: 50,000
- Time Period: January 2024 – December 2025
- Unique Customers: 7,992
- Data Type: Retail Transaction Data

Key Business Insights

Based on the analysis of the uploaded 50,000-row dataset:

1. Total Revenue: 109,299,235.77
2. Total Profit: 20,804,909.26
3. Profit Margin: 19.03%
4. Average Order Value: 2,185.98
5. Top Performing Category: Electronics, generating 71,296,955.43 in sales.
6. Top Performing City: Riyadh, generating 27,113,305.43 in sales.
7. Top Product by Revenue: Headphones, generating 14,663,247.26 in sales.
8. Most Used Payment Method: Card, with 23,897 transactions.
9. Repeat Customers: 7,899 customers made more than one purchase.
10. Yearly Sales Trend: Sales decreased by 2.92% in 2025 compared to 2024.

Business Recommendations

- Focus on Electronics to maintain strong revenue performance.
- Develop customer loyalty programs to retain repeat customers.
- Analyze Riyadh's sales performance and apply successful strategies to other cities.
- Promote high-performing products such as Headphones.
- Improve payment data collection to reduce missing values.
- Investigate the decrease in 2025 sales and develop strategies to improve revenue.
